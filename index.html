Bead 3 – Three Guti Game

Bead 3, also known as Three Guti or Three Bead, is a two-player traditional strategy board game. Move your three pieces across the board and create a winning line before your opponent.

How to Play Bead 3

  1. Choose Vs Computer to play against the AI.
  2. Choose Vs Friend for two players on one device.
  3. Select a piece to see its available moves.
  4. Move your piece to a connected empty point.
  5. Line up three pieces in a row to win the round.

Game Features

  • Free to play
  • Play against the computer
  • Two-player local mode
  • No installation required
  • Mobile-friendly
  • Works in any web browser
