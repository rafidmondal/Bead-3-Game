🎮 About Bead 3 Game

Bead 3, also known as Three Guti or Three Bead, is a free online strategy board game from the Raxzen game collection.

Inspired by the traditional Three Guti-style board game, Bead 3 combines a simple set of rules with a carefully designed premium tabletop-style interface, smooth interactions, responsive controls, sound effects, and a modern browser-based experience.

The game is designed to be quick to understand but strategic enough to make every move matter.

You can challenge the computer or play directly against a friend on the same device.

♟️ How the Game Works

Each player controls three pieces on a connected board.

The objective is to move your pieces strategically and create a winning line before your opponent.

During the game, players can:

  • Select their own piece
  • See available moves
  • Move pieces to connected empty positions
  • Plan their next move
  • Block the opponent
  • Create a winning line
  • Compete against the computer
  • Play locally with another person

The first player to successfully create a valid line of three pieces wins the round.

The game also includes a randomized first turn, so every new round can begin differently.

🤖 Play Against the Computer

Want to play alone?

Choose Vs Computer and challenge the built-in computer opponent.

The computer can:

  • Detect available legal moves
  • Look for an immediate winning move
  • Choose another legal move when a winning move is unavailable
  • Respond automatically during its turn
  • Continue the game without requiring another player

This provides a simple single-player experience while keeping the game lightweight and easy to start.

👥 Play With a Friend

Bead 3 also includes Vs Friend, allowing two people to play on the same device.

No second account or separate device is required.

Simply hand the device to the other player and take turns.

This makes it ideal for:

  • Friends
  • Family
  • Quick challenges
  • Offline-style local play
  • Short strategy matches
  • Casual entertainment

Bead 3 also includes Progressive Web App (PWA) functionality.

On supported browsers and devices, users can add the game to their home screen and use it with an app-like experience.
